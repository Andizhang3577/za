## 1. Most recent reported quarters (Q2 FY27, Q1 FY27) and Q3 FY27 guidance

### Takeaway
Q2 FY27 (reported Aug 27, 2026) set a record: revenue $2.739B (+37% YoY, +13% QoQ), a slight beat, with non-GAAP EPS of $0.94. Management guided Q3 to $3.15B ±5% (+15% QoQ, about +52% YoY) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.10 ±$0.05, well above the pre-print consensus of about $3.04B and $1.08. ## 3. Segment mix, customer programs, and growth drivers

### Takeaway
Marvell is now about 80% data center. Interconnect (electro-optics DSPs, the 1.6T ramp, scale-up optics) and switching drove FY27 so far. Custom silicon was about $1.5B in FY26, is guided to grow only about 20%+ in FY27 with most of the ramp in H2, and is expected to more than double in FY28. The expanded Google deal (August 2026) is the major new custom win, with a warrant structure that could represent up to about $120B in purchases over roughly 7 years. The communications and enterprise end markets are lumpy, with growth targeted near 10%.

### Inferences
- FY27 growth (about +46% to $12B from $8.195B) is driven mainly by interconnect and switching. Custom silicon becomes the larger driver in FY28 and FY29, so the optics and DSP cycle (800G to 1.6T) is the near-term swing factor, and execution on the H2 custom ramp is the next test.
- The Google warrant means some custom revenue effectively comes with equity dilution. It also concentrates exposure on one hyperscaler, while the AWS (Trainium) and Microsoft (Maia) program status stays unclear in public sources.
- The communications segment matters less each quarter (about 21% of revenue in Q2 and probably about 16% in Q3), so its weakness mostly dilutes the headline growth rate rather than signalling a problem in the core business.
