## 2. Bull case vs bear case (2026)

### Takeaway
Bulls argue for AI custom silicon (XPU) and optical/electro-optical interconnect growth, the Investor Day long-range targets (FY31), a ~20x forward P/E after a ~50%+ drawdown, and the strategic logic of owning Lumentum. Bears focus on Lumentum dilution (about one third of the company issued in stock), buying near an optical-cycle peak, a 2028+ close timeline, hyperscaler concentration and Trainium design-win doubts, and lower custom-silicon margins.

## 3. Valuation: forward P/E, EV/sales, PEG vs history and peers; FY27/FY28 consensus

### Takeaway
At ~$145, MRVL trades at roughly **20-22x forward earnings** (next-twelve-month or FY28 basis), **~48x trailing**, and **~14-15x EV/sales**. That is far below its own five-year average forward P/E of about 31-33x, and well below the 43-65x levels of mid and late 2026. It sits mid-pack among AI semis: above NVDA (~11-13x) and MU (~4x), similar to AVGO (17-22x) and CRDO (~20x), and below AMD (30-40x). On a PEG basis it screens cheap if FY28 EPS of about $6-7.6 is realised. However, the headline multiples exclude Lumentum dilution, and the EPS estimates in sources are inconsistent.
