## 1. Hyperscaler AI capex outlook (2026-2027) and what it means for custom ASIC and optical demand

### Takeaway
After Q3 2026 earnings (late Oct 2026), hyperscalers mostly held or nudged up their 2026 capex guidance: Alphabet $195-205B, Amazon ~$220B, Meta $135-145B. Microsoft's figure moved to ~$170B, with conflicting explanations. Explicit 2027 guidance is mostly qualitative. Alphabet signalled 2027 would be "significantly higher", and sell-side consensus implies roughly +17% to +42% growth by company. Nvidia's Nov 17 beat-and-raise confirms that demand is still strong at the top of the AI stack. The bear case on capex is about funding and returns (Oracle's debt, ROI doubts), not about cuts already announced.

## 2. Competitive position in custom AI accelerators (XPU/ASIC)

### Takeaway
Broadcom is still the clear leader in custom accelerators. Its confirmed XPU customers are Google, Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic and, reportedly new in 2026, Apple. Marvell is the No. 2 design partner, with a reported 20-25% share, anchored by AWS Trainium and Microsoft Maia. Whether Marvell holds the Trainium 3/4 sockets is disputed: Benchmark said it lost them to Alchip, while JPMorgan said management confirmed CY26 Trainium 3 purchase orders. Marvell's biggest 2026 win is a broad Google agreement signed Jul 29, 2026, tied to a warrant that vests up to $120B of Google revenue. It covers attach and inference products, not the core TPU, which Broadcom still holds.

### Cited Findings
- **Share:** Marvell reportedly holds 20-25% of the custom AI ASIC design-services market (Bloomberg Intelligence, as cited), anchored by AWS Trainium and Microsoft Maia wins. — [Absolute Digital Publishers](https://absolutedigitalpublishers.com/articles/marvell-the-other-custom-silicon-kingmaker); [HashrateIndex duopoly piece](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/design-partners-ai-asic-market-part-2/)
- **The Trainium 3/4 dispute, three views:**
  - Benchmark argued with a "high degree of conviction" that Marvell lost the Trainium 3 and Trainium 4 designs to Alchip.
  - JPMorgan wrote that Marvell management reiterated it had secured purchase orders for all of CY26 for the next-gen Trainium 3 XPU program.
  - A later analysis (X post, 2026) concluded Marvell is "very likely" in the Trainium 4 supply chain at the platform or subsystem level, but that public evidence does not establish it owns the main Trainium 4 compute-die socket. — [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/jpmorgan-said-marvells-management-told-them-their-microsoft-and-amazon-custom-chip-business-on-track-reports/); [TS2](https://ts2.tech/en/marvell-technology-mrvl-stock-slides-as-microsoft-and-amazon-fears-collide-with-a-bullish-ai-roadmap/); [TheValueist on X](https://x.com/TheValueist/status/2040169237172588647)
- **Trainium back-end partners:** reported as Marvell for Trn2 and Alchip for Trn3. One compiler flags these as reported, not confirmed. — [GitHub compilation](https://github.com/bmsdebug/chinascrape/issues/27)
- **Microsoft Maia:** the 3nm Maia XPU program with Marvell was reported on track for first deployment in 2H 2026, with volume in 2027. Earlier reports raised fears that Marvell could lose Microsoft and Amazon business, which JPMorgan disputed. — [TS2](https://ts2.tech/en/marvell-technology-mrvl-stock-slides-as-microsoft-and-amazon-fears-collide-with-a-bullish-ai-roadmap/); [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/jpmorgan-said-marvells-management-told-them-their-microsoft-and-amazon-custom-chip-business-on-track-reports/)
- **Google agreement:**
  - Signed Jul 29, 2026 and disclosed in a filing dated Aug 19, 2026.
  - Covers AI inference accelerators, storage controllers, NICs, memory interface controllers and near-memory compute.
  - Google received a warrant for up to 58.97M MRVL shares at $206.58 (about $12.18B if fully exercised).
  - One installment of 240 vests for every $500M of Google revenue Marvell recognizes for covered products, from Aug 1, 2026 to Jan 29, 2033, so $120B is the ceiling. Full exercise would add about 7% to the share count.
  - The agreement does not name TPU generations. Broadcom keeps the core TPU, and Marvell is focused on XPU-attach. — [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/mrvl-marvell-google-custom-chip-deal-warrant); [Converge Digest](https://convergedigest.com/marvell-google-tpu-custom-silicon-120-billion/); [Futurum Aug 20 2026](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/marvell-attaches-across-googles-tpu-stack-with-a-warrant-vesting-toward-120b/); [Techwire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2026/08/google-marvell-custom-ai-chips/)
- **Other Google partners:** Futurum lists MediaTek as a competitor on cost-optimized TPU variants. — [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/marvell-attaches-across-googles-tpu-stack-with-a-warrant-vesting-toward-120b/)
- **Broadcom and Meta:** Broadcom reportedly extended its Meta MTIA relationship (2nm, through 2029). — [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/meta-broadcom-ai-chip-deal-2029-mtia-2nm/)
- **Marvell's own outlook** (FQ2 FY27 call, Aug 27, 2026): FY2027 revenue about $12B (+45%) and FY2028 raised to about $18B. Data center revenue is expected up about 60% in FY27 and more than 60% in FY28. — [TradingKey transcript](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/transcripts/262137259-tradingkey); [Yahoo call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/marvell-technology-inc-mrvl-q2-050151485.html)

### Inferences
- The Google deal diversifies MRVL away from its AWS and Microsoft concentration. But the warrant structure means revenue arrives only as fast as Google buys, and it brings dilution risk on top of the Lumentum share issuance.
- The Trainium 3/4 question is still the largest unresolved competitive issue. Any December earnings commentary on the AWS program's continuity (Trainium 3 ramp, Trainium 4 role) will matter a lot.
- In-house design teams (AWS Annapurna, Google, Microsoft) plus Taiwanese back-end houses (Alchip, GUC) put a structural squeeze on MRVL's value capture on compute dies. That pushes MRVL toward attach, I/O and optics, which is consistent with the Lumentum deal.

## 3. Optical DSP and electro-optics competition, the 1.6T transition, and CPO risk

### Takeaway
Marvell is still a leading optical PAM4 DSP vendor, but share estimates conflict widely, from about 70% to about 40%. Several sources say Broadcom (3nm Taurus) and Credo (Cardinal 3nm, 200G-per-lane 1.6T now in volume) are gaining in 1.6T. One report says Marvell's 1.6T DSP needs a respin, with share recovery expected from about Q2 2027. Co-packaged optics (CPO) threatens pluggable DSPs over time. The Nov 2, 2026 Lumentum acquisition (all-stock, $115B EV) is Marvell's strategic answer: it buys lasers, transceivers and CPO capacity.

### Cited Findings
- **Share estimates conflict:**
  - One newsletter puts Marvell at about 70% of optical DSPs (1.6T "Ara" on 3nm).
  - Another says Marvell had about 60% in the 800G era, and that Broadcom is projected to reach about 60% by 2026, leaving Marvell about 40%.
  - These are newsletters, not Dell'Oro or LightCounting, so treat them as directional only. — [BEP Research](https://bepresearch.substack.com/p/the-quiet-architect); [WK substack](https://wukong123.substack.com/p/marvellasic-tpu-dsp-zr-optical-module); [Deep Fundamental](https://deepfundamental.substack.com/p/deep-dive-optical-module-market)
- **Credo:** volume shipments of 200G-per-lane 1.6T PAM4 DSPs and the launch of its Cardinal 1.6T family on 3nm. Reported as taking 1.6T share from Marvell. — [Yahoo/Zacks on Credo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/credo-sustain-momentum-optical-business-144500872.html); [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3870758441178373)
- **Marvell's 1.6T DSP:**
  - One report says it has performance weaknesses on some channels, with a respin underway, and expects share regained from about Q2 2027.
  - Another says the 1.6T DSP backlog runs about two years out. These two reports conflict. — [GTOAI substack](https://gtoai.substack.com/p/marvell-strategic-transformation); [BEP Research](https://bepresearch.substack.com/p/the-quiet-architect)
- **Market size:** the optical module DSP chip market was about $6.38B in 2025 (market-report site, lower confidence). — [SNS Insider](https://www.snsinsider.com/reports/optical-module-dsp-chip-market-11443)
- **Lumentum deal:**
  - Announced Nov 2, 2026. All-stock, implied EV $115B. 4.5 MRVL shares per Lumentum share, leaving MRVL holders with about 67% of the combined company.
  - Closing expected 1H 2028, subject to stockholder and US/China regulatory approvals.
  - Rationale: combine Marvell's electrical and optical interconnect, switching and custom XPU work with Lumentum's lasers and transceivers, positioning for co-packaged and near-packaged optics.
  - TrendForce (Nov 3, 2026) frames it as a potential challenge to Broadcom's leadership.
  - Futurum ties it to a projected $550B CY2030 AI connectivity TAM and to Lumentum's sold-out laser and CPO capacity. — [TrendForce Nov 3 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261103-13294.html); [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/marvell-pays-115-billion-for-lumentum-because-the-550-billion-ai-connectivity-tam-runs-on-lasers/); [Photonics.com](https://www.photonics.com/Articles/Marvell-Acquiring-Lumentum-for-115B/a72732); [optics.org](https://optics.org/news/marvell-set-to-acquire-lumentum-in-115bn-all-stock-deal); [SEC Form 425](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001633978/000119312526443841/d136689d425.htm)
- **Merchant-silicon risk:** TechTarget frames the deal as testing Marvell's merchant silicon business. Marvell would compete with optics customers who buy its DSPs. — [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/news/marvells-115-billion-lumentum-deal-tests-merchant-silicon-business/873486/)
- **Thesis:** a Korea Invest Insights note argues the bet is that lasers stay scarce even under CPO, because CPO cannot remove the laser. — [Korea Invest Insights](https://koreainvestinsights.substack.com/p/marvells-reported-lumentum-talks)

### Inferences
- The Lumentum deal hedges against pluggable DSPs being displaced by CPO. But it converts MRVL into a more vertically integrated optics player, which may alienate transceiver-maker customers such as Coherent, InnoLight and Eoptolink that buy its DSPs, opening a door for Broadcom and Credo.
- The 1.6T share trend through 2027 is a key debate. Conflicting reports mean the December earnings commentary on 1.6T ramps will be a catalyst.

### Gaps
- No authoritative Dell'Oro or LightCounting share data for 2026 was found.
- No quantified CPO adoption timeline was found (for example, Nvidia Spectrum-X/Quantum-X photonics or Broadcom Bailly volumes).
- No customer reaction (from transceiver makers) to the Lumentum deal was found.

## 4. The November 2026 Nvidia turmoil and the new US export licensing rule: effects on sentiment and MRVL

### Takeaway
The Nvidia events are confirmed by primary sources: an 8-K and press release, a filed class action, and the BIS rule.
- Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO, with board acceptance dated Nov 11, 2026 and the announcement on Nov 12. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey Jones is board chair.
- A securities class action was filed on Nov 9, 2026.
- BIS issued an interim final rule on Nov 16, 2026. It requires licenses for advanced AI chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, plus data-center and remote-access rules, effective about Nov 19.

The "six straight weekly declines" for Nvidia is not confirmed by what I found. MRVL fell with the sector (−7.3% on Nov 16), on top of its own Lumentum overhang. Nvidia's strong Nov 17 results gave some relief after hours.

  - Kress (CFO since 2013) is interim CEO; Harvey Jones was named the first-ever board chair. — [NVIDIA 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm); [NVIDIA newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition); [Al Jazeera Nov 12 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/why-did-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resign-and-who-is-colette-kress)

  - Interim final rule issued Nov 16, 2026; effective on Federal Register publication, about Nov 19.

  - Existing data centers have 90 days to obtain a license.
  - Data centers, foundries and exporters must do ownership due diligence. Applications are reviewed case by case.
  - Fortune links the rule to the Nvidia scandal and the Singapore and Malaysia diversion concerns. — [Fortune Nov 16 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/11/16/new-commerce-rule-nvidia-chip-singapore-malaysia-china/); [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/826051); [Quartz](https://qz.com/us-ai-chip-reexport-licenses-five-countries-111626); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/tech/40073620)

China's Foreign Ministry called it "economic coercion" (Nov 18, 2026). — [TechTimes Nov 18 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330982/20261118/beijing-calls-us-chip-export-rule-economic-coercion-malaysia-tells-data-centers-comply.htm)

- **Effect on MRVL:**
  - MRVL fell 7.3% on Nov 16, 2026 (S&P 500 −1.9%), with no company news. 33 of 34 other semiconductor names above $2B also fell.
  - Fool attributes the chip weakness to Huang's departure and to de-risking before Nvidia's earnings.
  - TradingKey and Fool also cite the Lumentum deal's dilution overhang. — [Trefis Nov 17](https://www.trefis.com/stock/mrvl/articles/625792/why-did-marvell-stock-fall-over-7/2026-11-17); [Fool Nov 16](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/16/why-marvell-stock-is-down-today/); [TradingKey Nov 16](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262307698-market-movers-mrvl-20261116)
- **Other MRVL moves that week:** −4.38% on Nov 12 and −6.23% on Nov 13. — [TradingKey Nov 12](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262302927-market-movers-mrvl-20261112); [TradingKey Nov 13](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262305064-market-movers-mrvl-20261113)

### Inferences
- MRVL's direct exposure to the BIS rule is probably limited. Its custom XPUs go mostly to US hyperscalers, which are generally exempt. But Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand are big hubs for assembly, test and data centers, so the due-diligence, data-center and remote-access provisions add compliance friction and sentiment risk for all AI chip suppliers.
- The Nvidia turmoil mainly works through sentiment and correlation: a governance and China-espionage narrative plus pressure for tighter rules. Nvidia's strong FQ3 print and guidance undercut a "demand is cracking" reading. Nvidia's US-China political exposure could also, at the margin, push hyperscalers further toward custom silicon, which would be a mild positive for MRVL and AVGO. That is speculative.

## 5. Semiconductor sector performance in 2026 and MRVL versus peers

### Takeaway
2026 has been a boom-and-bust year for chips. The SOX rallied about 100%+ to a record on June 22, 2026, after a violent early-June shakeout (−10.3% on Jun 5). It fell into a bear market by Jul 17, about 20% off the high and about 28% by late July per a lower-quality source. It then recovered, and was reported at +57% YTD in October. MRVL outperformed massively through mid-October: +214% YTD at about $266, compared with AVGO +3%. It then lost about half its value in a month, driven by the Lumentum deal (Nov 2) and the November chip selloff. By Nov 13 it was about +81% YTD at about $154, against AVGO about +0.1% and AMD about +144%.

### Cited Findings
- **SOX peak:** all-time closing high on Jun 22, 2026, reported as 14,634.72 by KuCoin and 14,655 by another source (a minor conflict), after rising more than 100% YTD. — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/when-will-the-philadelphia-semiconductor-index-sox-bottom-out); [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/chip-stocks-crash-2026/)

## 6. Upcoming catalysts and key risks

### Takeaway
The next hard catalyst is MRVL's FQ3 FY27 earnings on Dec 1, 2026. The guide is $3.15B ±5% revenue, non-GAAP EPS of $1.05-1.15, and data center revenue up more than 20% sequentially. Investors will focus on:
- the AWS Trainium continuity question,
- the Maia ramp,
- the Google revenue pace,
- 1.6T DSP share, and
- Lumentum deal messaging.

The main risks are:
- a long overhang from the Lumentum deal, which issues about 33% of the combined share count to Lumentum holders and doesn't close until 1H 2028, including a China SAMR review;
- customer concentration;
- export-control escalation;
- macro risk (a possible Fed hike in Jan 2027);
- AI capex funding and ROI doubts.
